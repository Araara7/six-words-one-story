A complete story in exactly six words, for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun.

## The story

> Grandma's last recipe called for forgiveness.

Six words, 45 characters. A whole life implied: grandma is gone, her last recipe was not for food but for forgiveness — the family rift, the death, and the reconciliation are all left for the reader to fill in.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically (two runs produce byte-identical output).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
